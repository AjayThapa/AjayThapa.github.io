I started in Kathmandu, spent six years on the core production team at Paradygm TV with Sisan Baniya, and since 2023 I've edited remotely for 40 Days Production in the US.

The whole time I've kept my own channel running alongside the client work. I'm now based in Sydney with full Australian working rights, and I'm available immediately.
